# Google-Extended is not a crawler, it is a switch: it decides whether pages
# Googlebot already fetched may be used by Gemini - both for training and for
# grounding an answer in the Gemini app. It does not affect Search, and AI Overviews
# read Googlebot's copy either way. Refusing it would cost citations in Gemini and
# protect nothing Google does not already hold, so it is allowed.
